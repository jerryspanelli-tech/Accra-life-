# Emergency vehicle model sources

The civic replacement uses these externally hosted GLB models:

- **Police SUV base:** City SUV, Car Park and Road Vehicle Fleet
  - Model: https://cdn.3dassets.dev/assets/32519/v1/model.glb
  - Asset page: https://3dassets.dev/assets/car-park-and-road-vehicle-fleet-city-suv-427b6f75
  - Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal
  - 29,436 triangles; nominal dimensions 1.933 × 1.64 × 4.062 m.
- **Ambulance base:** Ambulance, Fire and Emergency Services
  - Model: https://cdn.3dassets.dev/assets/29900/v1/model.glb
  - Asset page: https://3dassets.dev/assets/fire-and-emergency-services-ambulance-f22ce1a5
  - Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal
  - 4,744 triangles; nominal dimensions 3.371 × 2.76 × 6.006 m.

Both assets are described by their publisher as free for commercial use under CC0 1.0, with no attribution required. Accra Life adds its own Ghana Police / Ghana Health Service livery overlays and emergency light bars at runtime.

These are generic vehicle models, not licensed Toyota manufacturer models or official Ghana Police / Ghana Health Service vehicles. The in-game liveries are fictional game artwork.
